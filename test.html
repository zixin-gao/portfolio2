Automatic Card Dealer

This PCB runs an automatic card dealer: an ESP32‑S3 chip controls the motors to deal cards, uses an IR sensor to detect each card, and shows system status on a small OLED screen. A buck converter on board keeps the motors running smoothly, and users can change dealing modes either by turning a physical rotary encoder or wirelessly over WiFi.
